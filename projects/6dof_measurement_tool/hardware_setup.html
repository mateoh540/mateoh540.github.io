Hardware Setup

The LEDs were mounted using a custom rigid frame with batteries providing power.

Two cameras were mounted on either side of the XPlanar mover to capture the LED positions from different angles. The cameras were calibrated using the TwinCAT vision library to obtain the rotation matrix and intrinsic parameters.
